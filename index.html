About me

I am an econometrician working on econometric theory, statistics, and empirical finance. I will join the School of Economics at Peking University as an Assistant Professor in September 2026.

My research develops methods for high-dimensional covariance estimation, conformal inference, U-statistics under cross-sectional dependence, factor models, and network dependence.
